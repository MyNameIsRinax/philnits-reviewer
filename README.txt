IT PASSPORT (ITPEC IP) EXAM REVIEWER
====================================

How to use
  Open the site on GitHub Pages, or serve this folder with any static web server
  (for example: python -m http.server) and open index.html.
  Keep the img folder next to index.html.

Exams included (100 questions each, 900 total)
  April 2026, October 2025, April 2025,
  October 2024, April 2024, October 2023, April 2023, October 2022, April 2022

Ways to study
  - Smart practice: 20 questions mixing ones you have not seen with your past mistakes.
  - Review mistakes: every question whose latest answer was wrong. Answer it right to clear it.
  - Flagged: questions you flagged to come back to.
  - Practice by topic: Technology, Management or Strategy.
  - Past exams: go through a full paper in order, with a results page at the end.
  - Mock exam: 100 questions, 120-minute timer, answers hidden until you submit,
    then a pass estimate (60% overall and at least 30% in each topic).
  - Custom set: pick exams, topics and how many questions.

While answering
  - Every choice shows an explanation after you answer.
  - "Shuffle answer choices" (Settings) reassigns the letters A-D.
  - Keyboard: A-D or 1-4 answer, Enter next, Left/Right move, F flag, G question list.
  - Tap the question image to enlarge it.
  - Light and dark themes; question images adapt to the theme.
  - Progress is saved in your browser. Progress from the previous version is carried over.

Notes on the answer keys
  - October 2025 Q42: the official key lists "Reinstalling the web browser". This reviewer marks
    "Cutting off connection with the wireless LAN, and isolating the PC" as correct, since that is
    the standard first step; the explanation mentions the discrepancy.
  - April 2026 Q64 and Q71 misprint their choice letters in the PDF (e.g. "e)"); choices are
    shown in printed order as A-D.

Files
  index.html, style.css, app.js  - the site
  data.js                        - answers, topics and explanations
  img/                           - question and choice images, one folder per exam
  img/philnits-logo-mono.png     - navbar logo (one color, recolored for dark mode)
